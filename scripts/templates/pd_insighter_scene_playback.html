Alignment checks & limits

Cyan tint lies on the reconstructed appearance, not on a floating rectangle. It estimates the central rectified 90-degree camera region using two Gaussian depth passes (0.3-12 m axial range). Conflicting depths and depth edges stay uncolored. Gaussian depth is approximate: holes, transparent splats and missing occluders can still cause errors. This is not confirmed physical visibility, eye gaze, object recognition or measured surface area. The Original point frame is preserved independently.

Gold video boundary = the matching rectified crop, not the entire fisheye image. Optional diagnostic dots use sparse MPS patches: cyan = candidate, coral = center, gray = unsupported. They do not verify occlusion. Video-texture projection is not enabled.
