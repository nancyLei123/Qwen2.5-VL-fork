Read sharpness and completeness together

A sharp texture is not proof of correct geometry. Gaussian Splatting may look smoother and more complete, while a mesh may expose holes or warped surfaces. Inspect chairs, door edges and steps, not only flat walls.

What this test does, and does not, tell us

Metashape Professional 2.3.2: fixed Aria MPS poses and rectified intrinsics; image matching and triangulation; 1024-pixel depth estimation; a triangle mesh; full-resolution source texture blending into a 4096-pixel atlas. No mesh or texture hole filling.

Controlled comparison: both methods use the same frame set, excluding the same reference image from their reconstruction optimization. This does not test independent Metashape camera alignment. Only one reference image per scene is evaluated, and MPS geometry plus Gaussian initialization may contain information from the full recording. It is an exploratory comparison, not an end-to-end held-out benchmark.

Privacy: images, models and results stay on this Mac. This page uses local files and local viewer libraries; no cloud processing or external analytics.
