Methodology: what Aria supplies, what we reconstruct, and how texture works

Inputs and meanings

Aria fileRole
semidense_points.csv.gzEstimated XYZ landmarks in meters. Semi-dense does not mean every surface is covered.
closed_loop_trajectory.csvDevice position and orientation over time in the same world frame. This page uses position.
semidense_observations.csv.gzWhich camera saw a point, at what time and pixel. Useful for coverage and visibility evidence; not necessarily an RGB observation.
online_calibration.jsonlCamera optics and camera-to-device transforms over time. Required for accurate color projection.
.vrs + .vrs.jsonThe original VRS holds camera frames, sensors and timestamps; its JSON sidecar holds recording metadata, not a 3D mesh.

Current pipeline

Quality and spatial filters retain scene evidence. A 0.14 m voxel density field, Gaussian smoothing, and marching cubes estimate an envelope around those points. The extraction stride is 0.28 m; neither number is an accuracy guarantee.

How far can we go now?

We can inspect a partial route-level scene, large surface groupings, floor-level changes and where the head moved. We cannot yet claim complete rooms, identified furniture, reliable stair dimensions, calibrated RGB appearance, or clinical obstacle measurements.

Recovering texture, in plain language

  1. Read an original RGB frame and its exposure time from VRS.
  2. Find the device's position and orientation at that exact time.
  3. Account for where the RGB camera is mounted on the glasses.
  4. Calculate which image pixel corresponds to each 3D surface point.
  5. Reject hidden surfaces, points behind the camera and blurred/bad views.
  6. Combine reliable colors from several views instead of painting from one frame.
  7. Save vertex colors first, or a UV texture atlas for more detailed appearance.

Our existing undistorted MP4 is useful for review, but does not provide the exact frame timing and output camera projection needed for calibrated texturing. The separate Aria environment now reads Ntrial3 VRS, and the local RGB experiment uses original frames. Coloring cannot recover missing geometry.
