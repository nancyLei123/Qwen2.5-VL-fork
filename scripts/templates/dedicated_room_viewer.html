What changed for this reconstruction

Frames were screened across the entire recording for sharpness and camera-pose coverage. Original RGB pixels were rectified at 1408 x 1408. MPS closed-loop poses, online calibration and confidence-filtered semi-dense points preserve one metric coordinate system.

Person / phone masks and a small near-body exclusion strip reduce foreground contamination. Masked pixels are ignored, not treated as transparent holes. Point colors use training views only. The training schedule reaches 30,000 steps, with 12 fixed held-out views and a one-second temporal exclusion around them.

Automatic masks are imperfect and can also exclude static objects, such as decorative heads. Glass, reflective metal, low-texture walls, moving objects and unobserved surfaces remain difficult. More recording time does not guarantee complete room geometry. This is research context, not validated clinical measurement.

Input audit / Camera and image provenance / Foreground-mask review / Train / validation split / Methodology

Project Aria Tools, OpenSplat, GaussianSplats3D and a local YOLO11 segmentation model. Training and numerical validation use SH3; this installed browser renderer displays up to SH2, so the live view is not pixel-identical to the offline renders.

MPS geometry and poses are estimated from the entire recording. The evaluation measures held-out RGB appearance conditional on those poses, not independent geometric accuracy. The held-out views are development validation, not a final untouched test set.

Same-view quality comparison

The actual image and both checkpoints use the same measured camera pose. Background scores ignore the foreground masks; full-frame scores include people and phones and are reported separately.
